The premise

Your book stays yours.

Traditional publishing asks an author to hand over the thing they made in exchange for help making it. We don't think that trade is necessary. We do the work a publishing house does — editorial, design, production, the unglamorous logistics — and you keep what a publishing house usually takes.

That isn't a loophole or a technicality. It's the whole arrangement, written down at the start of the project.

  • Full rightsThe work is yours to license, adapt, reprint, or walk away with.
  • The final sayEvery decision reaches you with our recommendation already attached. We tell you plainly what we would do and why. Then you choose, and nobody you have never met gets a vote.
  • Your creative directionThe voice, the vision, and what the book is for are yours and stay yours.
  • Our professional judgmentEight years of production experience goes into the parts a reader notices only when they are wrong — typography, structure, pacing, the cover, the quiet conventions of a finished book. We consult on every one of them so that what you publish holds its own beside anything from a major house.

How we work

Personalized, in the literal sense.

  1. 01

    We start with your goals

    Every project begins with an individual consultation. Not a package to pick from — a conversation about what this book is for, who it needs to reach, and what success actually looks like to you.

  2. 02

    We build a real timeline

    Thoughtful and realistic, which are the same thing. You'll know what happens when, what we need from you, and what we're doing while you wait.

  3. 03

    We bring in the right people

    We work with a team of trusted professionals — editors, designers, proofreaders, production — so the finished book is the whole package and not just a manuscript in a cover.

  4. 04

    We pave the way out

    A book's placement in the world is planned long before it gets there. Metadata, categories, format, timing, and the practical path to the readers it was written for.

Through all of it, the project is managed and you are supported. You should never have to wonder where your book is.
